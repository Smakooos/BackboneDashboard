About the project

A practical internship dashboard built to turn data into insight

This project is a web-based dashboard designed to present backbone network data in a clear, interactive, and professional way. It helps transform raw information into understandable visuals, summaries, and downloadable reports that can support analysis and decision-making.

It is part of a two-week internship at Djeezy, supervised by Sofiane Saidi. The main goal of this experience was to discover how technical data can be organized, interpreted, and presented through a real-world web application. The project aims to strengthen analytical thinking, improve presentation skills, and demonstrate how modern tools can be used to simplify complex information.

About me

I am Bouzid Noureddine, a student at ESI, the Higher National School of Computer Science, located in Algiers. I am passionate about networks, technology, and the practical side of software development.

I created this dashboard as a first immersive experience in building a complete product that combines analysis, design, and implementation. This internship gave me the opportunity to explore a professional environment and better understand how ideas become useful digital solutions.

Future vision
