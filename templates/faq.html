Quick intro

Developer: Bouzid Noureddine

Age: 20

Location: Algeria

Focus: Networks, web development, and data-driven solutions

I am Bouzid Noureddine, a 20-year-old student at ESI in Algeria. I live in Algeria and I am passionate about networks, technology, and building practical solutions. I created this dashboard as a first immersive experience in the world of real projects, where I could combine learning, design, and technical implementation.

This project is a modern dashboard created to present backbone-related data in an elegant and understandable way. It includes visual summaries, analytics, and report generation features that make it easier to interpret technical information.

This dashboard was developed during a two-week internship at Djeezy, under the supervision of Sofiane Saidi. The internship aimed to explore how data can be analyzed and transformed into a useful digital experience through a practical and professional project.

ESI, the Higher National School of Computer Science, is one of the most prestigious engineering schools in Algeria. It is known for its strong focus on computer science, software engineering, and advanced technical education. It prepares students to excel in the digital and technological world.

The dashboard was built using Flask for the web application, Bootstrap for the interface, and Python for data processing and analysis. The design aims to be simple, modern, and professional while staying functional and easy to navigate.
